Q4 Company Rock 1

How we'll share our story, every month

This is our plan for Rock 1, building a marketing engine. Here is how one good idea turns into something people watch, read, and share, and how we learn from it. Every month we do it two ways: once for future clients and once for current clients.

🤖 AI helps at every step. 🧑‍💼 A person checks and decides at every step.

    Then we do it again

    What we learn this month shapes next month's topics. Your ideas can come in any time through the Zoom chat or by forwarding an email. The goal is a simple routine we repeat, not a one time push.

    A few words, in plain English

    Ideal clients
    The kinds of people we serve best. Marketers call this an ICP.
    Content
    Anything people can watch, read, or listen to: podcasts, videos, articles, posts.
    Brief
    A short plan for one topic: the main point and who it is for.
    Paid ads
    Paying to show our content to more of the right people. Also called paid media.
    Reminder ads
    Ads shown to people who already visited our site, so they come back. Also called retargeting.
    Scorecard
    A weekly report card that shows what is working.
    Funnel
    The path from first hearing about us to becoming a client.
