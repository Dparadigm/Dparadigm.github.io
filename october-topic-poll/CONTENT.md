### 2. Policy Loans Without Interrupting Growth
**Simple**
- Audience: New business
- ICP: Sovereign CEO
- What's in it: Follows one real policy loan into an outside purchase and back, showing cash value keeps growing the whole time. Introduces the loan mechanic from scratch for people who've never seen it. (Compare existing-client live walkthrough later — same mechanic, on a real client account.)
- How it's used:
  - Podcast (primary)
  - Blog post
  - Newsletter
  - Social clips through LinkedIn and Instagram
  - Email sequence

**More depth**
- Expanded what's in it:
  - Follow one dollar of capital through a policy loan into an outside asset purchase and back
  - Show what the cash value does during the loan — the mechanic prospects hear asserted and never see demonstrated
  - Position policy loans as foundational architecture inside the 4-3-2-1 framework, not an isolated hack
- Light example: Show the cash-value ledger continuing to credit growth in the same month a $50K loan funds a real estate down payment.
- Why this ICP: Sovereign CEO language is “financing ourselves as both the bank and the borrower” and “velocity of money” — this is that mechanic demonstrated.
