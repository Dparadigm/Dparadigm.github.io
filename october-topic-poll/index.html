Simple
Audience
New business
ICP

What's in it
Follows one real policy loan into an outside purchase and back, showing cash value keeps growing the whole time. Introduces the loan mechanic from scratch for people who've never seen it. (Compare existing-client live walkthrough later - same mechanic, on a real client account.)
How it's used
Podcast (primary) - transcript becomes a blog post, which informs the newsletter; episode also cut into social clips across LinkedIn/Instagram/X; could extend into an email sequence
More depth
Expanded what's in it
  • Follow one dollar of capital through a policy loan into an outside asset purchase and back
  • Show what the cash value does during the loan - the mechanic prospects hear asserted and never see demonstrated
  • Position policy loans as foundational architecture inside the 4-3-2-1 framework, not an isolated hack
Light example
Show the cash-value ledger continuing to credit growth in the same month a $50K loan funds a real estate down payment.
Why this ICP
Sovereign CEO language is “financing ourselves as both the bank and the borrower” and “velocity of money” - this is that mechanic demonstrated.
