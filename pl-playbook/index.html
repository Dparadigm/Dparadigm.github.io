Garrett's edge isn't any one ad. It's a system. One face records one weekly sit-down. That recording gets cut into about 4 clips a day, a weekly live, blog posts, and podcast audio, and his paid ads point to free offers (a free book where you pay shipping, a free digital book, an overview video) that feed email, quizzes, and an application (cadence.md, ads-research.md). We already have most of the raw inputs: Patrick, a 482-episode podcast, a book with a confirmed free-plus-shipping offer (not live yet), the 4-3-2-1 whitepaper, a live WealthScore, and Family Office 360 in build. The score is our quiz and qualifier, and it already works today. Family Office 360 could be the next step after the score, the way Garrett's Report of Findings follows his qualifier. Until FO360 opens, a waitlist captures that next step honestly.

Could copy: the cadence, the repurposing, the single face, the offer ladder, the score-then-next-step flow, the pre-launch waitlist, the one-question qualifier, and tracking on organic posts. Shouldn't copy: absolute claims ("safer than banks," "untouchable," guarantees), scarcity that isn't real ("Founding Member spots are limited" unless it's true), and the sameness. Garrett, Wealth Factory, and the Infinite Banking crowd all sell Rockefeller, family bank, and "be your own bank." Our lane could be measurable architecture: the 4-3-2-1 system, a score, and a ranked Gap Report. "Our product is architecture" (Patrick, Rally, Oct 19) is the line that sets us apart.
