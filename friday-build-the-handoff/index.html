Build the handoff

The situation

An email just came in: an in-force policy request asking for policy and illustration materials, wanted today.

It looks like a client request. It is not. It is from sales.

So "just send the files" is the wrong move. Client-facing materials going out to the wrong person, unreviewed, is the failure mode here.

The outcome

A complete spec. Someone who wasn't in your group could read your board and know exactly what this thing does.

  • One job, stated in plain words
  • Only the tools it actually needs
  • A real person who owns it next
Your job

In your group, spec the thing that handles this email. Pick what kind of system it is, what it's allowed to use, what starts it, what it reads, what it does, and who owns it next. Write the instruction in your own words. Then submit for a grade.

Group
Group members

The board

Tap a slot, then a piece. Or tap a piece and it lands in the open slot.

Your group

The pieces

One container. Two to four tools. One piece each for trigger, input, action, and handoff. You write the instruction.

Pieces

Submit for a grade

Every slot needs a piece and the instruction needs words. You'll get Success or Try Again, plus the reason why.

Fill out the board. Submit for a grade.
