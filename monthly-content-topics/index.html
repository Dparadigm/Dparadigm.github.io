Month walkthrough

This is the conversation path: what happens, in order.

1

Start with the audience (Cornelius)

We ask our Trinity ICP agent for a fresh pack: segments, pains, and the language the market uses. Topic ideas have to connect back to that, not only a brainstorm from the calendar.

Owner: Cornelius · Input for: CMO
2

Build the option list (CMO)

Our Trinity marketing agent proposes 6–10 options (podcast, and/or newsletter + live stream), each with a one-line reason tied to the ICP. Your calendar is a constraint and a timing filter , not the only source of ideas.

Owner: CMO · Review: Rachel + Dan
3

Human pass

You and Dan do a light edit for brand, voice, and anything Patrick’s calendar already commits. Keep the open-ended write-in for strategists.

Owner: Rachel · Support: Dan
4

Wealth strategists force-rank (Zoom)

We post a Zoom Workplace Poll: rank the options, add anything missing, and mark co-host interest. Close window is short (for example 48 hours).

Audience: Wealth strategists · Poll run: Rachel (Dan sets the first template)
5

Lock top topics and produce

Use the ranks (plus write-ins) to lock the top set for the month. Align timing with sales if needed. Then produce and publish on your existing path.

Owner: Rachel · Align as needed: Patrick / Mike
6

Measure and feed next month

After publish, CMO returns a short performance snapshot. That informs next month’s option list, still grounded in Cornelius ICP.

Brief: CMO · Scoreboard row: Rachel

Zoom Workplace Poll (what strategists see)

Delivery is Zoom Workplace Polling, not Slack, not an email survey.

Example: October podcast

  1. Force-rank: “Rank these October podcast topic options (1 = most relevant for your clients).”
  2. Open-ended: “Anything missing the market is talking about that should beat these?”
  3. Co-host: “Which of these would you co-host or want to be involved in?” (multi-select; optional “none this month”)

If Zoom can’t do a true ranking poll, we fall back to “pick your top 3,” then “which is #1,” plus the same open-ended and co-host questions , so you’re never stuck on the tool.

Who does what

Clear lanes so nothing depends on one person reinventing the process each month.

Rachel

Approves the system, light-edits options, runs the monthly poll after the first template, locks topics, produces, owns the scoreboard row.

Dan

Drafts the mechanics once, helps set the first Zoom poll template, partners on the Wednesday package and the first human pass.

Cornelius (Trinity)

ICP source of truth: who we serve and what they care about this month.

CMO (Trinity)

Turns ICP + performance history into the option list; returns the post-publish measurement brief.

Wealth strategists

Force-rank topics and signal co-host interest so production matches what clients actually need.

Patrick / Mike

Align on locked topics and timing when needed; calendar constraints feed the human edit step.
