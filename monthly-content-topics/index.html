How we choose monthly topics

A simple loop so podcast, newsletter, and live-stream topics come from who we serve, then wealth strategists help rank what to make next, then you produce and we measure what worked.

Q4 rock context

This is the working shape of a Q4 marketing rock: a repeatable monthly system for choosing specific, useful topics (by policy, need, audience, and ICP), ranking them with wealth strategists via two Zoom polls (new business and existing clients), producing across channels, and measuring what worked.

Two equal tracks: new business (podcast-led) and existing clients (newsletter + live streams). Existing-client content has to add real value after purchase, not feel like another acquisition pitch. You own approval and execution. Dan is drafting the mechanics with you so month one is clear.

Two Zoom Workplace Polls (what strategists see)

Delivery is Zoom Workplace Polling, not Slack, not an email survey. Two polls each cycle (new business; existing clients) with the same question pattern, run 2-3 weeks before the content month. Each poll shows short titles only; full topic cards live on a companion one-pager linked from the Zoom Team Chat message that opens the poll. October content may already be locking by 2026-09-25; the first ahead-of-month dual polls in this rock are for November (~2026-10-08).

Example poll questions (per track)

  1. Force-rank: “Rank these topic options (1 = most relevant for your clients). Cards: [link].”
  2. Open-ended: “Anything missing the market is talking about that should beat these?”
  3. Co-create: “Which would you co-host, demo, or want to be involved in?” (multi-select; optional “none this month”)
  4. Resources: “For any of these, do you already send an article, checklist, competitor piece, or Dropbox resource we should know about?”
  5. Tools: “Is there a calculator, spreadsheet, Truth Concepts demo, workbook, or third-party app you use to make this personal for a client?”

If Zoom can’t do a true ranking poll, we fall back to “pick your top 3,” then “which is #1,” plus the same follow-on questions, so you’re never stuck on the tool.

Idea intake (sales → marketing)

Polls are one input. Advisors also need an easy place to dump competitor emails, analogies, client questions, and links without writing an essay.

Home base

Dedicated Zoom Team Chat channel

Sales ↔ marketing collaboration space. Drop links, forwards, voice notes, and poll activity in one place. This is the primary intake path.

Also useful

Email forward

Optional dedicated inbox so advisors can forward competitor or client emails without expecting a reply every time. Marketing (and later agents) sort and use it.

No content hub until this simple path works. Collecting is only valuable if we turn ideas into shipped content. Showing that submissions get used is how participation sticks.

Example card (sample only, not a real October lock):

Title (what shows in the poll): Why existing clients still need a policy review after year one

  • Channel: Newsletter (could also seed a live stream later)
  • ICP: Primary = post-purchase household still optimizing cash flow
  • What is in it: What changed in year one; three review questions to ask; when to book a strategist vs self-serve

Every card should include:
