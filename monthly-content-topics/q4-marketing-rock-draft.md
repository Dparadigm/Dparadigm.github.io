Prompt: “Rank these November topic options (1 = most relevant for your clients). Cards: [link].”

#### Question 2 — Open-ended
“Anything missing the market is talking about that should beat these?”

#### Question 3 — Co-host
“Which of these would you co-host or want to be involved in?” (multi-select)

#### Question 4 — Resources
“Do you already send clients something on any of these (article, analogy, competitor forward, Dropbox piece)? Paste or describe.”

#### Question 5 — Tools
“For any of these, is there a calculator, Truth Concepts demo, spreadsheet, exercise, or third-party app you’ve used that made it click?”
